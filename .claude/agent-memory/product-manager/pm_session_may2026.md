Key decisions from May 2026 PM session:

- Client confirmed all 10 sections. Page flow order is finalized (see HANDOFF.md).
- Three missing sections: FAQ accordion, Location + Hours, Patient Portal CTA.
- Market research (#1 pain: too many clicks, #2: mobile broken, #3: overwhelming) directly drives simplification mandate.
- Mobile-first polish is as important as adding missing sections.
- Contact form stays in the Contact section; Location + Hours gets its own dedicated section above FAQ.
- Standalone red CTA banner removed — replaced by Patient Portal CTA section.

**Why:** Client confirmed missing sections reduce support load (FAQ) and increase portal adoption (Patient Portal CTA). Location prominence addresses #4 client complaint.

**How to apply:** Every feature decision should reduce friction for first-time Filipino patients booking on mobile. Trust signals (doctor credentials, testimonials, clear address) are high-priority.
